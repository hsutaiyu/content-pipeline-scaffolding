Lines from Jack’s and Andreas’s research calls, as of Oct 12, 2026

These are the reusable lines from Jack's and Andreas's research calls, sorted into our three content buckets plus the research program.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| The Line column is ready to use. | It is verbatim from the call with filler removed ("it's like", "right?", false starts), with house style applied: Internet capitalized as a noun, curly apostrophes, no em-dashes. Where a line broke a rule in company/voice (https://app.coconut.md/pages/company/voice), the Edit column says what changed, and “Built from” marks a line written around the speaker’s own phrase. |
| First person runs from that person's handle. | A line in Andreas's or Jack's own "I" goes on their handle, in their words; the company handle gets the "we" version where one is given. |
| Researcher words are signal, not copy. | Nothing a researcher said goes public without their written consent. Their lines sit in What's landing and Questions researchers ask. |
| Lines marked "notes" | were quoted in Day AI's call notes, not read off the transcript. Check the wording against the recording before it ships. |
| Numbers keep their conditions. | Andreas's scale figures are back-of-envelope estimates, and the Edit column says so wherever a line carries one. |
| No fellowship dates or amounts. | Both moved several times across these calls, so lines about the program leave them out. |
| The name is moving. | Calls through mid-September say IOV or Internet of Value; October calls introduce the Open Value Initiative. Lines say "we" wherever a name would date them. |
