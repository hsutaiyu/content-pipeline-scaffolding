# Bucket 4 Research program

<!-- Snapshot of the “Low level content” sheet (research-call lines, as of Oct 12, 2026). Ported 2026-10-21. -->

Three framings carry the fellowship on every call: curated review instead of blind submission, negative results welcome, and everything open. Dates and amounts are left out on purpose.
