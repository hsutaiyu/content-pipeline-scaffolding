# Swift and Chainlink interoperability experiments (background)

**What happened:** Swift ran experiments with more than a dozen institutions, including ANZ, BNP Paribas, BNY Mellon, Citi, Clearstream, Euroclear, Lloyds, SIX Digital Exchange and DTCC, plus Chainlink. They moved simulated tokenised assets between public chains, and between public and private chains, using Swift connectivity. Swift noted that institutions otherwise need a separate connection to each ledger.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries; fragmentation as the outcome of many bundled ledgers.

**Post angle:** Before Swift built its own ledger, it tested whether it could connect everyone else's. Its finding was that each new ledger is one more connection every bank has to maintain.

**Caution:** 2023 and an experiment. Chainlink has a token; leave it out. Use this as context for item 8, not on its own.
