# American Express backs purchases made by registered agents

**What happened:** Amex launched the Agentic Commerce Experiences (ACE) developer kit (agent registration, card enablement, "intent intelligence", tokenised credentials, cart context sharing) and Agent Purchase Protection, which covers eligible Card Members for charges arising from a registered agent's error. Amex said it would use its existing dispute processes.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance. Intent and cart context are captured so Amex can investigate disputes inside its own network. A closed-loop network can hold all sides of the record; open systems cannot.

**Post angle:** Amex says it will absorb the cost when a registered agent gets a purchase wrong, and builds intent capture to make that decision. A clear actor, decision and consequence.

**Caution:** The Business Wire release returned 403; the five ACE components are from search snippets of that release. Protection has eligibility conditions; do not describe it as blanket cover.

**Source:** "American Express releases tools to build AI payments—and pledges to pay the price if agents go awry", Fortune, Jack Kubinec, 14 April 2026
