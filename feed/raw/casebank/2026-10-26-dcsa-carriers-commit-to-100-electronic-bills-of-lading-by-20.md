# DCSA carriers commit to 100% electronic bills of lading by 2030

**What happened:** Nine carriers (MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd, ONE, Evergreen, Yang Ming, HMM, ZIM) committed to 50% eBL within five years and 100% by 2030. DCSA said about 45 million bills of lading are issued a year and only 1.2% were electronic in 2021, and estimated $6.5 billion in direct savings.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries; competing platforms must interoperate so a document can move between them with a single holder.

**Post angle:** Competitors that together move most of the world's containers agreed on one standard for a document none of them owns.

**Caution:** 2023 source; look for a 2025-26 DCSA progress update before using the 1.2% figure as current.

**Source:** "DCSA's member carriers commit to a fully standardised electronic bill of lading by 2030", DCSA, 15 February 2023
