# EU Digital Product Passport Registry goes live

**What happened:** Under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (Article 13), the Commission launched the central DPP Registry on 20 July 2026. The registry holds unique product identifiers and metadata only; the passport data itself stays decentralised with the economic operators (manufacturers, importers or their service providers). The first deadline is 18 February 2027 for certain large batteries.

**Thesis tie:** Very close to event-state dissociation by regulation. A thin shared index records that a passport exists and where; each operator keeps and is responsible for the content under its own authority.

**Post angle:** Brussels built a registry that deliberately does not hold the data. Each manufacturer keeps its own records, and the EU keeps the index that lets anyone find them.

**Caution:** Do not suggest the Commission design is incomplete or needs Open Value. Present it as institutions already choosing a minimal shared layer.

**Source:** "The Digital Product Passport Registry is now live", European Commission (DG GROW), 20 July 2026
