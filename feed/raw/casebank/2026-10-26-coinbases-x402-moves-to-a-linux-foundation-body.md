# Coinbase's x402 moves to a Linux Foundation body

**What happened:** x402, the HTTP 402 payment protocol Coinbase released in May 2025, is now stewarded by a vendor-neutral x402 Foundation with 40 founding members. Premier members are Adyen, AWS, American Express, Circle, Cloudflare, Coinbase, Fiserv, Google, Mastercard, Monad Foundation, MoonPay, Ripple, Shopify, Solana Foundation, Stellar Development Foundation, Stripe and Visa.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance, handled the other way round: the protocol was moved out of one company's control into neutral governance so competitors would adopt it.

**Post angle:** Coinbase built an agent payment protocol, then handed it to a neutral foundation, and Visa, Mastercard and Stripe joined. Neutral governance was the condition for adoption.

**Caution:** x402 settles largely in stablecoins on public chains; stay away from token or price talk. Usage figures circulating (e.g. "165 million transactions") come from secondary sites and are UNVERIFIED. Coinbase's own launch page returned 403.
