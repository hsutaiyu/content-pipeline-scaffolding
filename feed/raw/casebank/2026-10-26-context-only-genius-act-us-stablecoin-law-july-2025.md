# Context only: GENIUS Act (US stablecoin law), July 2025

**What happened:** Signed 18 July 2025. Issuers must hold one-to-one reserves, segregate them, publish monthly reserve composition reports examined by a registered public accounting firm, and have the CEO and CFO certify those reports. The law takes effect at the earlier of 18 months after enactment or 120 days after final regulations.

**Thesis tie:** Even when a token's ledger is public, the law requires a separate, off-chain attestation that the reserves match it. That is a reconciliation between a blockchain record and bank and custody records, made mandatory.

**Post angle:** The on-chain record doesn't settle the question by itself; a monthly reconciliation with an accountant's examination does.

**Caution:** HIGH. No price or token talk, and no suggesting Open Value relates to stablecoins. Use only as a recordkeeping example.
