# EU Digital Identity Wallets due by end of 2026 (eIDAS 2.0)

**What happened:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (in force 20 May 2024, per search results) requires every Member State to offer at least one wallet, built to common specifications, by the end of 2026; search results give 24 December 2026, with regulated relying parties required to accept wallets about a year later. Five implementing regulations were adopted on 28 November 2024. Each state issues its own wallet, and public and private bodies issue attestations into it.

**Thesis tie:** Sovereign domains deriving their own state. Twenty-seven issuers, many attestation providers, one set of shared rules for verifying across borders.

**Post angle:** By Christmas 2026, a bank in Lisbon should be able to accept an identity issued by Estonia. Each country keeps its own register; what they share is how to check it.

**Caution:** Identity is sensitive; avoid surveillance or "single ID" framing. Confirm the 24 December date in the Official Journal text before using it.
