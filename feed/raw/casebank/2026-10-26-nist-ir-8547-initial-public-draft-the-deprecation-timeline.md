# NIST IR 8547 (initial public draft): the deprecation timeline

**What happened:** NIST's draft roadmap identifies the quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms in its standards and maps them to the FIPS 203/204/205 replacements. Per the draft as summarised in multiple search results, RSA and elliptic-curve algorithms at roughly 112-bit security are to be deprecated after 2030 and all quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms disallowed after 2035.

**Thesis tie:** Post-quantum foundations; long-lived records. A signature on a 2026 record may need to be re-verified after 2035.

**Post angle:** NIST has written down the year RSA and elliptic-curve signatures stop being acceptable in its standards. Every registry that signs records now has an end date on its signatures.

**Caution:** It is still an initial public draft. Verify the 2030/2035 wording against the PDF before quoting it as fact. Search results also mention a June 2026 executive order and OMB memo that cite these dates; I could not verify those, so do not use them.

**Source:** NIST IR 8547 ipd, "Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards", NIST CSRC, 12 November 2024
