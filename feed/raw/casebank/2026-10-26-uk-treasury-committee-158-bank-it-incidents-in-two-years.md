# UK Treasury Committee: 158 bank IT incidents in two years

**What happened:** After a Barclays outage that began on 31 January 2025 and lasted up to three days, the Committee wrote to nine banks and building societies. Their replies showed at least 158 IT incidents between January 2023 and February 2025, more than 800 hours of unavailability. Barclays reported the most (33). Barclays said it would pay £5 million to £7.5 million in compensation for the January outage.

**Thesis tie:** Context for how often a bank's derived state becomes unavailable or out of date. Weaker on cross-boundary reconciliation specifically.

**Post angle:** Use as a scale statistic only, alongside items 7 and 11.

**Caution:** Easily slides into "banks are broken" framing; use it sparingly and neutrally. Check exact figures on the page by hand before publishing.
