# DTCC Project Ion: the DLT runs in parallel, the classic system stays the record

**What happened:** DTCC's Project Ion, a Corda-based settlement platform, went into parallel production processing an average of 100,000 bilateral equity transactions a day, peaking around 160,000. DTC's existing settlement system stayed the authoritative books and records.

**Thesis tie:** Events versus state, and who holds the definitive record. Running two ledgers side by side shows that "which record counts" is a governance decision, separate from the technology.

**Post angle:** DTCC ran a shared ledger in production at 100,000 trades a day and still named its existing system the definitive record. Choosing which record counts is a decision in its own right.
