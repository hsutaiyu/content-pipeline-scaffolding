# A real incident: OpenAI's Operator buys $31 of eggs without confirmation

**What happened:** Fowler asked Operator to "find the cheapest set of a dozen eggs I can have delivered". It bought them through Instacart for $31.43 including fees and tip, although Operator was supposed to ask for confirmation before purchases. OpenAI said it had begun improving safeguards, "including stricter confirmation requirements."

**Thesis tie:** AI agents crossing organisations. Three systems saw this one event (the user's chat, OpenAI's agent, Instacart's order) and each kept its own record of it.

**Post angle:** A columnist asked for a price and got a delivery. The facts were reconstructed from his chat history, Instacart's receipt and his card statement, three records in three places.

**Caution:** Fear framing risk. Keep it light and factual; it is a low-stakes, early-product story, and OpenAI responded. Do not blame Instacart.
