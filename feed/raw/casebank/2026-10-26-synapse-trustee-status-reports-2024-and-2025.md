# Synapse trustee status reports (2024 and 2025)

**What happened:** The court-appointed trustee, former FDIC Chair Jelena McWilliams, put the shortfall in "for benefit of" accounts at $65 million to $96 million. The 2024 report noted Evolve Bank's view that "any single bank cannot complete full reconciliation without more collaborative work across the Synapse ecosystem." By April 2025 the trustee had stopped trying to reconcile, citing cost and the difficulty of interpreting Synapse's data and systems.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries with no neutral record. Each bank saw only its own slice. A full reconstruction needed data from every party, and when the cost of that became too high the work stopped before it reached the last dollar.

**Post angle:** A trustee with full legal authority still could not reconstruct the full sequence of money movements, because the record of what happened was split across institutions that each held only part of it.

**Caution:** The trustee's reports are court filings; quote them, don't characterise them. Avoid the "missing money" fear framing. Evolve's statements are those of a party in litigation.
