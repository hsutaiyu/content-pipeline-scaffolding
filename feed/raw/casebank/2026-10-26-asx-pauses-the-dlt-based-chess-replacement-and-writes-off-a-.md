# ASX pauses the DLT-based CHESS replacement and writes off A$245-255 million

**What happened:** After an independent review by Accenture, ASX paused its replacement for CHESS, Australia's equities clearing and settlement system, which was being built with Digital Asset on distributed ledger technology. The review estimated the application was 63% complete. It flagged complexity "in the way ASX requirements interact with the application and underlying ledger" and vendor-management issues between ASX and Digital Asset. ASX said the existing CHESS "remains secure and stable, and is performing well."
ASX moved to a product-based replacement (TCS BaNCS, chosen Nov 2023) delivered in two releases. Release 1 (clearing) went live on 20 April 2026 with all approved market operators connected. Markets Media reports that Release 2 (settlement and subregister) is targeted for 2029 and that costs are expected within a A$125 million estimate.

**Thesis tie:** Coupling between business rules and the ledger. The review pointed to the interaction between the market's requirements and the underlying ledger. This is the "recording bundled with execution" issue, here inside a single operator.

**Post angle:** Australia's exchange spent years rebuilding its settlement record on a shared ledger and then stopped. Its independent reviewer pointed to how the market's rules were interwoven with the ledger underneath.

**Caution:** A litigation tail exists (see 5b). Do not frame this as "DLT failed" or blame Digital Asset. The ASX release lists several causes, not one.

**Source:** "ASX will reassess all aspects of the CHESS replacement project and derecognise capitalised software of $245-255 million pre-tax in 1H23", ASX market announcement, 17 Nov 2022
