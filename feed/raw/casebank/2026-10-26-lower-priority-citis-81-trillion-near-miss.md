# Lower priority: Citi's $81 trillion "near miss"

**What happened:** In April 2024 Citi credited $81 trillion instead of $280 in a transfer between two internal ledger accounts. Two employees missed it, a third caught it about 90 minutes later, and it was reversed hours later with no funds leaving the bank. It was reported to the Fed and OCC. Citi had 10 near misses of $1 billion or more in 2024.

**Thesis tie:** Weak for this area: it happened inside one institution, not across a boundary. It shows the difference between an intended event and a recorded state, and detective controls working.

**Caution:** Ridicule magnet. Probably skip it, or use it only to show controls doing their job.

**Source:** CBS News, "Citi mistakenly credits $81 trillion to customer account," 28 February 2025 (reporting an FT story)
