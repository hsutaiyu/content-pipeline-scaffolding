# ESMA statement on settlement-fail penalties after the T2S/T2 outage

**What happened:** The outage delayed central securities depositories' reporting for 27 February until early on 28 February, which delayed participants' own reconciliation and caused fails over the following days. Knock-on effects reached the international CSDs and currencies beyond EUR and DKK. ESMA said national authorities agreed that all fails on 27 and 28 February in all EEA CSDs were affected and that cash penalties should not apply.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries. When the shared infrastructure that supplies the reference record pauses, every downstream participant's books fall behind, and the regulator has to set aside normal rules for the days affected.

**Post angle:** Actor: ESMA and national regulators. Decision: waive penalties for two days EU-wide. Consequence: an official acknowledgment that participants couldn't settle until they could reconcile.

**Caution:** Pair with item 7 and keep the tone neutral. Not a story about blame.

**Source:** ESMA Public Statement ESMA74-2119945926-3232, "On the treatment of settlement fails with respect to the CSDR penalty mechanism, following the major incident that affected T2S and T2 on 27 February 2025," 14 March 2025
