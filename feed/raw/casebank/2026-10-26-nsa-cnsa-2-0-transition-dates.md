# NSA CNSA 2.0 transition dates

**What happened:** NSA's schedule for US national security systems: software and firmware signing should prefer CNSA 2.0 by 2025 and use it exclusively by 2030; browsers, servers and cloud services exclusively by 2033; the overall transition complete by 2035, in line with NSM-10.

**Thesis tie:** Post-quantum foundations, with signing named first. Signatures over records are where the migration starts.

**Post angle:** The US national security community chose code-signing as its first post-quantum deadline. Integrity of records comes before secrecy of traffic.

**Caution:** National-security framing can drift into fear or militarism. Use dates only, and confirm them in the PDF before publishing.

**Source:** "Announcing the Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite 2.0" (CSA, updated May 2025), NSA
