# EU Deforestation Regulation: postponed, simplified, then reviewed

**What happened:** Parliament and Council agreed on 4 December 2025 to delay application to 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and 30 June 2027 for micro and small ones. The revision puts the due diligence statement on the operator who first places goods on the market, with only the first downstream operator keeping the reference number. On 4 May 2026 the Commission's review estimated annual compliance costs falling from €8.1 billion to €2.0 billion and proposed no further amendments.

**Thesis tie:** Events vs state across a supply chain. Plot-level geolocation evidence is recorded once upstream; each downstream party relies on a reference rather than repeating the work.

**Post angle:** The EU cut the number of companies that must re-file the same deforestation evidence. One record upstream, a reference number downstream.

**Caution:** Politically contested (delay, NGO criticism). Stay neutral; do not take sides on the delay.
