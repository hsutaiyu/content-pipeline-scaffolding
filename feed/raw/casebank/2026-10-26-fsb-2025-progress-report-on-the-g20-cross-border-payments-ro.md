# FSB 2025 progress report on the G20 cross-border payments roadmap

**What happened:** Five years into the G20 roadmap, the FSB said most roadmap actions were complete but had "not yet translated into tangible improvements for end-users at the global level," and that meeting the 2027 targets globally was unlikely. Wholesale payment speed and remittance delivery improved, but costs and transparency did not. The CPMI brief, drawing on responses from 82 jurisdictions, points to interoperability by design, ISO 20022 alignment, standard APIs and longer operating hours as the foundations.

**Thesis tie:** Sovereign domains each running their own systems; coordination across them is the bottleneck, not technology inside any one of them.

**Post angle:** Actor: the FSB. Decision: say plainly that the targets won't be met on schedule. Consequence: the focus moves to jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction implementation and interoperability.

**Caution:** Don't spin "unlikely" into "failed." The FSB frames it as a call for implementation.

**Source:** Financial Stability Board press release, "FSB calls for enhanced policy implementation to achieve tangible improvements in cross-border payments," 9 October 2025
