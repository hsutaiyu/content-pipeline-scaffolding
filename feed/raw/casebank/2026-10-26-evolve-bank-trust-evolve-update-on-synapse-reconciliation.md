# Evolve Bank & Trust, "Evolve Update on Synapse Reconciliation"

**What happened:** Evolve says it hired Ankura Consulting and used Federal Reserve and its own transaction data to find errors in Synapse's ledgers, then found more end-user funds and scheduled further payouts. It says the other partner banks (AMG, Lineage, American) had not produced the data needed to finish reconciling.

**Thesis tie:** The plainest statement in this whole set of the problem shape: no single party held the full event history.

**Post angle:** Use the quote as a framing line about the general problem of multi-party records, attributed to Evolve.

**Caution:** HIGH. This is a party to the litigation, and the statement assigns blame to Synapse and the other banks. Evolve itself was under a Federal Reserve enforcement action. Use only the structural sentence, attributed, and never as a verdict on who was at fault.

**Source:** Evolve Bank & Trust media release, early 2025 (it schedules disbursements "on or about March 6, 2025")
