# EU coordinated PQC implementation roadmap

**What happened:** The roadmap follows a Commission recommendation of 11 April 2024. Per multiple summaries found in search: Member States start national transitions and inventories by end of 2026, high-risk systems (critical infrastructure, finance, government) move to PQC no later than end of 2030, and as many systems as feasible by 2035. Hybrid classical plus post-quantum schemes are recommended where suitable.

**Thesis tie:** Post-quantum foundations across sovereign domains. Twenty-seven states migrating on one timetable is itself a coordination problem.

**Post angle:** The EU asked every Member State to start its post-quantum move in the same year that each must also launch a digital identity wallet. Two cross-border foundations, one calendar.

**Caution:** The landing page itself does not list the milestones; cite the roadmap PDF for exact wording.

**Source:** "A Coordinated Implementation Roadmap for the Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography", European Commission with Member States (NIS Cooperation Group), 23 June 2025
