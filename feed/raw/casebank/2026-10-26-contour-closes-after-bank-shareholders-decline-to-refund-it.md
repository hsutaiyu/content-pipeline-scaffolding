# Contour closes after bank shareholders decline to refund it

**What happened:** Contour was a letter-of-credit network with nine bank shareholders (Bangkok Bank, BNP Paribas, Citi, CTBC, HSBC, ING, SEB, SMBC, Standard Chartered) and 22 bank members. It announced that it would stop operating on 30 November 2023 after a funding round for two more years of runway failed. GTR reports that no shareholder would act as lead investor and that venture capital found the ownership structure unwieldy. Users had to finish or migrate transactions and download their data before access ended.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance. When the record goes, each participant has to pull its own copy out before the deadline. That is the exit cost of a record that lives inside one operator.

**Post angle:** Contour gave 31 banks one month to download their trade records. Its shared ledger had cut letter-of-credit processing from days to under 24 hours in tests (per GTR). It closed because nine co-owners could not agree on who would lead the next round.

**Caution:** Contour was later acquired (by Xalts, then involving XDC Ventures, per Trade Finance Global and GTR headlines, not fetched). Stay away from that token-linked follow-up and any price talk.
