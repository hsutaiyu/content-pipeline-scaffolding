# we.trade runs out of cash

**What happened:** we.trade was a blockchain trade-finance platform owned by 12 European banks and IBM, among them HSBC, Deutsche Bank and Santander. GTR reports that it ceased operations after failing to agree further funding with its shareholder banks. In 2020 it had laid off about half its staff, and in 2021 six member banks put in €3 million to keep it going. Member banks would handle existing customer trades themselves, off the platform.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance and funding. When the shared record is a company owned by its users, its survival depends on a dozen shareholders agreeing to keep paying.

**Post angle:** Twelve banks co-owned one shared trade record. When they could not agree to fund it, the record closed and each bank went back to handling its own customers' trades.

**Caution:** This is from 2022, older than ideal. Keep the focus on ownership structure, not "blockchain failed".

**Source:** Eleanor Wragg, "we.trade calls it quits after running out of cash", Global Trade Review, 6 Jun 2022
