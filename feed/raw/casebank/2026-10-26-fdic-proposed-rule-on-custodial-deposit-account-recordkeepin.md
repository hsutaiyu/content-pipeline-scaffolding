# FDIC proposed rule on custodial deposit account recordkeeping (Sept 2024) and its status

**What happened:** In direct response to Synapse, the FDIC proposed that banks holding pooled custodial accounts with transactional features keep records of each beneficial owner and balance, have "direct, continuous, and unrestricted access" to any third party's records, reconcile daily, and get annual independent validation. Status: still a proposal. It was not withdrawn in March 2025, but I found no final rule or re-proposal as of October 2026. Treat the status as "proposed, not finalized" and recheck before publishing.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries; who holds the record. The regulator's answer is to make the bank hold or reach a mirror of the third party's ledger and reconcile it every day. That is the coordination work institutions do today, written into proposed rules.

**Post angle:** Actor: the FDIC. Decision: require daily reconciliation and continuous access to a partner's records. Consequence: the bank inherits responsibility for keeping two ledgers in agreement.

**Caution:** Do not say the rule is in force. Avoid partisan framing about the leadership change at the FDIC.

**Source:** FDIC, Statement by Chairman Martin J. Gruenberg on the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, 17 September 2024
