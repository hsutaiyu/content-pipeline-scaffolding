# T+1 After Action Report (SIFMA, ICI, DTCC)

**What happened:** The US moved equities settlement from T+2 to T+1 on 28 May 2024. Nearly 95% of transactions were affirmed by the 9:00 pm ET trade-date cutoff, up from 73% at the end of January 2024. The NSCC clearing fund fell by an average of $3.0 billion (23%), from $12.8 billion to $9.8 billion. The July 2024 CNS fail rate averaged 2.12% and the DTC non-CNS fail rate 3.31%, both in line with T+2 levels.

**Thesis tie:** Neutral ordering and reconciliation under time pressure. Affirmation is the step where buyer, seller and custodian confirm they agree on the trade, and T+1 squeezed that agreement into trade date.

**Post angle:** Actor: US market participants. Decision: agree on every trade's details on the same day. Consequence: a 22-point jump in same-day affirmation, and less margin tied up. A positive story about coordination working.

**Caution:** None major. Frame as success. Fail-rate numbers are a single month.

**Source:** SIFMA press release, "SIFMA, ICI, and DTCC Release 'T+1 After Action Report'," 12 September 2024
