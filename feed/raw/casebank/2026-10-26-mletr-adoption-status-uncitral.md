# MLETR adoption status (UNCITRAL)

**What happened:** UNCITRAL lists legislation based on or influenced by the Model Law in 13 jurisdictions, including Bahrain (2018), Singapore (2021), ADGM in the UAE (2021), the UK (2023), France (2024), and China (2025, bills of lading only). Search results report Germany's draft remained unadopted as of mid-2025.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across borders. A bill of lading crosses several legal systems on one voyage; each recognises it under its own law.

**Post angle:** A ship can leave a port where an electronic bill of lading is legal and arrive where it is not. Thirteen jurisdictions have aligned so far.

**Caution:** Adoption list changes; recheck the page on publication day.

**Source:** Status: UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (2017), UNCITRAL
