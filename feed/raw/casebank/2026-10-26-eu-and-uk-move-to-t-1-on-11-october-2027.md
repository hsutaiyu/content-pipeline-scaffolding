# EU and UK move to T+1 on 11 October 2027

**What happened:** ESMA recommended 11 October 2027 for a synchronised EU move to T+1 across all relevant instruments. The CSDR amendment is now law and applies from 11 October 2027 (search results and the ESMA page also mention some provisions phasing in from December 2026). The UK chose the same date, backed by HM Treasury, the FCA and the Bank of England.

**Thesis tie:** Multiple sovereign domains (US, EU, UK, Switzerland) aligning their timing so cross-jurisdiction trades don't fall into gaps. Each keeps its own rules and market structure while coordinating on when an event becomes final.

**Post angle:** Actor: EU and UK authorities. Decision: move on the same day, a year from now. Consequence: firms that settle across both markets get one cutover instead of two mismatched cycles.

**Caution:** Exact phasing of individual EU provisions should be rechecked against the regulation. No fear framing about the 2027 deadline.

**Source:** ESMA, Final Report on shortening the settlement cycle, 18 November 2024
