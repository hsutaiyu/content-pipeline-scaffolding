# NIST finalises the first post-quantum standards (FIPS 203, 204, 205)

**What happened:** NIST published FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, key encapsulation), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, signatures) and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, hash-based signatures). NIST urged administrators to start integrating them at once because migration takes years.

**Thesis tie:** Post-quantum foundations. Records meant to be relied on for decades (titles, credentials, trade documents) carry signatures that must stay verifiable after today's algorithms are retired.

**Post angle:** NIST's own lead on the project said the hard part is time, not maths. Any shared record built today inherits that clock.

**Caution:** No "quantum apocalypse" framing. Do not put a date on a cryptographically relevant quantum computer.

**Source:** "NIST Releases First 3 Finalized Post-Quantum Encryption Standards", NIST news release, 13 August 2024
