# EMVCo drafts a framework for card-based agentic payments, with shared "Intent Services"

**What happened:** The card industry's standards body (owned by the major networks) published a draft "EMV Agentic Payments – Framework for Specifications" from a new Agentic Payments Task Force, open for comment until 30 September 2026. It proposes "Intent Services", a shared layer for participants to "register, reference, retrieve and manage consumer-authorised intent before, during and after a transaction", and is exploring Know Your Agent checks and Agentic Transaction Indicators. EMVCo is working with the FIDO Alliance, OpenID Foundation, OpenWallet Foundation and W3C.

**Thesis tie:** Neutral ordering and shared records. This is the industry itself saying intent should be held in a layer that several participants can reference across the life of a transaction. The closest real-world echo of the thesis in this area.

**Post angle:** In September the card industry's standards body proposed a shared place for "consumer-authorised intent" that issuers, merchants and agents can all reference after the fact. Comment period closed 30 Sept; worth watching what comes next.

**Caution:** Draft only. Do not imply EMVCo is building anything like Open Value or that Open Value is relevant to its work. PYMNTS follow-up (2 Oct 2026, https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/emvco-framework-targets-consumer-intent-agentic-payments/, opened) adds that indicators are meant for "risk assessment, fraud prevention, dispute resolution".

**Source:** "EMVCo Requests Feedback on Framework for Secure, Interoperable and Scalable Card-Based Agentic Payments", EMVCo, 1 September 2026
