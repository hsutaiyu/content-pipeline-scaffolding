# UK Electronic Trade Documents Act 2023, section 2

**What happened:** The Act came into force on 20 September 2023 and lets electronic bills of lading, bills of exchange and warehouse receipts carry the same legal effect as paper. To qualify, a "reliable system" must identify the document, protect it from unauthorised change, ensure only one person controls it at a time, and remove the previous holder's control on transfer. The Act does not name a technology.

**Thesis tie:** Neutral ordering and single-holder records across organisations. The law specifies the property (one controller, in sequence) and leaves the system open.

**Post angle:** English law defined what makes a digital bill of lading real without choosing a platform. The test is about who held it and in what order.

**Caution:** Legal text; do not paraphrase the criteria loosely.
