# OpenAI pulls back from Instant Checkout six months later

**What happened:** OpenAI scaled back in-chat checkout and moved toward merchant apps (Walmart, Sephora, DoorDash) that complete purchases on the retailer's own systems. Walmart's Daniel Danker described results as "disappointing", with in-chat conversion three times lower than click-outs to Walmart's site. Retailers wanted real-time inventory, loyalty integration, coupons and store pickup, which the first version lacked.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries. The agent's view of price and stock and the merchant's view diverged, and the merchant's system won. Shows that each domain keeps its own state and wants to.

**Post angle:** OpenAI built checkout into the chat; retailers took it back to their own sites. The hard part was keeping two systems' view of one order in step.

**Caution:** Not a story of failure to mock. Corroborated by CNBC (24 Mar 2026, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html, 403 when fetched, confirmed via search) quoting OpenAI: "the initial version of Instant Checkout did not offer the level of flexibility that we aspire to provide".
