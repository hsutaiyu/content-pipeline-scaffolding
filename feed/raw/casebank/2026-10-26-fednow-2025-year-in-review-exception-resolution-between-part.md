# FedNow 2025 year in review: exception resolution between participants

**What happened:** Nearly 1,600 financial institutions were on FedNow, volume grew about 460% year over year, and nearly 30,000 transactions ran per day. New features include an Exception Resolution Service that lets participants "directly communicate with other participants," and a $10 million transaction limit.

**Thesis tie:** Even a brand-new, always-on central-bank system ships with a dedicated channel for two institutions to sort out disagreements about a payment. Reconciliation is designed in, not bolted on.

**Post angle:** Actor: the Federal Reserve. Decision: build exception handling into the network. Consequence: an admission that instant settlement doesn't remove the need to resolve disputes between participants.
