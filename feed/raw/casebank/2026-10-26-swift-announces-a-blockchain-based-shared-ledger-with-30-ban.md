# Swift announces a blockchain-based shared ledger with 30+ banks, now at MVP build

**What happened:** At Sibos in September 2025, Swift said it would add a shared ledger, with Consensys building a prototype and more than 30 banks involved in design. It described the ledger as one that will "record, sequence and validate transactions and enforce rules through smart contracts", focused first on 24/7 cross-border payments. By March 2026, Ledger Insights reported that more than 40 institutions had joined the design. The MVP is expected to carry live tokenised-deposit payments before the end of 2026, acting as an orchestration layer on existing rails, with interbank settlement initially through RTGS or correspondent banking.

**Thesis tie:** Neutral ordering and events versus state, as institutions are building it now. Swift's own wording separates "record, sequence" from "validate" and "enforce rules". The ledger is a shared view of commitments, and each bank's balances stay at the bank.

**Post angle:** The banks' own cooperative is building a shared record of what each bank committed to and in what order. Final settlement is left to existing rails. That choice says a lot about which layer the industry wants to share.

**Caution:** Ledger Insights' governance details are paywalled; do not assert who operates the ledger. The ledger is built on Linea (an Ethereum layer 2) according to secondary sources; avoid any token angle. Do not position Open Value as an alternative or competitor to Swift.
