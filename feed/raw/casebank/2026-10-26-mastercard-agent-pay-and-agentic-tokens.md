# Mastercard Agent Pay and Agentic Tokens

**What happened:** Mastercard introduced Agentic Tokens, built on its existing tokenisation (card-on-file, mobile wallets, Payment Passkeys), plus agent registration and verification. Launch collaborators were Microsoft (Azure OpenAI, Copilot Studio), IBM (watsonx Orchestrate, for B2B use) and Braintree and Checkout.com. The release's business example is a small textile company using an agent to source international suppliers and pay them.

**Thesis tie:** AI agents crossing organisations, and specifically the B2B case the thesis cares about: an agent finding a supplier abroad and paying across independent systems.

**Post angle:** Mastercard's own launch example is a textile business whose agent sources and pays foreign suppliers. Each side of that payment keeps its own record; the network token ties them together for this one transaction.

**Caution:** Rollout timeline (Citi/US Bank pilots, all US cardholders by Nov 2025) came from a secondary site (eco.com) and is UNVERIFIED against a Mastercard primary source.

**Source:** "Mastercard Unveils Agent Pay, Pioneering Agentic Payments Technology to Power Commerce in the Age of AI", Mastercard investor relations, 29 April 2025
