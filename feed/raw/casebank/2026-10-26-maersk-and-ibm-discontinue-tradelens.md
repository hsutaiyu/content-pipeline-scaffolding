# Maersk and IBM discontinue TradeLens

**What happened:** Maersk and IBM announced that they would withdraw TradeLens, a shipping data platform launched in 2018 and jointly developed by IBM and GTD Solution, a Maersk division. Maersk said it had been founded "as an open and neutral industry platform" but had not reached commercial viability as an independent business. The platform was due to go offline by the end of Q1 2023.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance. The record and its operator came as one package, and when the operator is a competitor, joining the record means trusting the competitor.

**Post angle:** Maersk built a working shared record for global shipping and called it neutral. Four years later it closed the platform, saying that full industry collaboration had never come. The software was fine. The open question was whose platform it was.

**Caution:** The "competitors refused to join a rival's platform" story is widely repeated, but Maersk never said so. Attribute it as analyst interpretation (Ti Insight, ABI Research) and include Lyon's caveat. Avoid any framing that mocks Maersk or IBM. Do not imply a different architecture would have saved it.
