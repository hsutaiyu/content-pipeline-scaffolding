# Marco Polo Network enters insolvency

**What happened:** The Irish High Court appointed Interpath Advisory as provisional liquidators to Marco Polo Network, a trade-finance network built on R3's Corda that had more than 30 bank members. GTR reports debts of €5.2 million. A proposed US$12 million strategic partnership with Bank of America fell through in late January 2023.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance. More than 30 banks used a shared record held by one small operating company, and its finances decided whether the record continued.

**Post angle:** Thirty banks were on one trade-finance network. When one funding deal collapsed, the company behind the shared record went into liquidation within weeks.

**Caution:** This is an insolvency court proceeding, so keep to the reported facts with no blame. Do not cite the FTX link as cause unless attributed (some outlets connect the two; GTR's text as fetched does not establish it).

**Source:** Eleanor Wragg, "Marco Polo brings in liquidators as funds run dry", Global Trade Review, 23 Feb 2023
