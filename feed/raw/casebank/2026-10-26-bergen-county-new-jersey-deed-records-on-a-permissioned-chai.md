# Bergen County, New Jersey: deed records on a permissioned chain

**What happened:** Ava Labs and Balcony are digitising about 370,000 property records (reported value $240 billion) across Bergen County's 70 municipalities on an Avalanche-based chain, with a claimed 90%-plus cut in deed processing time. The county keeps control over who can change and see records.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance, stated by a blockchain company's own president. The public authority will not hand its rules to a network.

**Post angle:** Even the chain's builder says the county cannot give up ownership of its registry. Adoption depends on keeping the authority where it already sits.

**Caution:** Vendor-reported numbers; university news story, not a county filing. Avoid naming tokens.

**Source:** "Blockchain platform securely digitizes public records", Cornell Chronicle, 4 August 2025
