# DTCC picks Canton for tokenised Treasuries and takes a governance seat

**What happened:** Following an SEC no-action letter, DTCC said it would let a subset of DTC-custodied US Treasuries be minted on the Canton Network. It targeted an MVP in a controlled production environment in the first half of 2026. DTCC also became co-chair of the Canton Foundation's governance alongside Euroclear.

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance, approached from the other side. Before relying on a shared network, the largest post-trade infrastructure in the US took a seat in governing it.

**Post angle:** When DTCC put Treasuries on a shared network, it also asked to co-chair the body that governs it. Joining a record and governing a record arrived as one decision.

**Caution:** Canton has a native token; stay away from it entirely. Do not take a side in the public debate over Canton's transparency. Do not claim the MVP went live unless confirmed.
