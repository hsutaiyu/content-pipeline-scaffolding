# US: Senator Warner's AI AGENT Act asks for agent action records and NIST standards

**What happened:** S. 5051, the "Artificial Intelligence Access, Gatekeeper Exchange, and Nondiscriminatory Transfer Act of 2026", sponsored by Sen. Mark Warner, was introduced on 21 July 2026 per GovInfo and referred to the Senate Commerce Committee. Goodman reports that it would require "custodial user agents" to "maintain real-time records of actions they take on the user's behalf" and bars them from delegating to another agent without "express and revocable permission." Search results summarising the bill text say it directs NIST to develop standards for verifying delegation and keeping auditable records (bill text on congress.gov returned 403: UNVERIFIED).

**Thesis tie:** Events vs state; AI agents crossing organisations. Legislators are asking for a record of what agents did, and the commentary already points out the record would sit with each agent provider.

**Post angle:** A US senator wants every agent that acts for you to keep a log of what it did. The open question is what happens when your agent's log and the merchant's log disagree.

**Caution:** Date discrepancy: Tech Policy Press says introduced 29 June 2026; GovInfo says 21 July 2026. Use GovInfo's date or say "summer 2026". Bill is in committee; do not imply it will pass. Avoid partisan framing.
