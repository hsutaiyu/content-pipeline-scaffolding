# EU battery passport from 18 February 2027 (Battery Regulation 2023/1542)

**What happened:** From 18 February 2027, EV, light-means-of-transport and industrial batteries above 2 kWh placed on the EU market need an electronic battery passport, reached by QR code. The economic operator placing the battery on the market is responsible for the data, and access differs by role (public, recyclers, authorities). When a battery is repurposed or remanufactured, a new passport follows it, linked to the original.

**Thesis tie:** Reconciliation across boundaries and over time. A battery passes from carmaker to second-life operator to recycler, each its own authority, yet the history has to remain one coherent sequence.

**Post angle:** A car battery can outlive the company that made it. The EU now requires its record to follow it to the next owner and the one after that.

**Caution:** Article-level details (repurposing link, access tiers) must be checked in the EUR-Lex text before use.
