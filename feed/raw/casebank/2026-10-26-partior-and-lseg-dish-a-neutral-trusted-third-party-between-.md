# Partior and LSEG DiSH: a "neutral, trusted third party" between bank payment ecosystems

**What happened:** Partior is a bank-owned shared-ledger payments network whose shareholders include DBS, J.P. Morgan, Standard Chartered, Temasek, Deutsche Bank and Emirates NBD. It is working with LSEG's Digital Settlement House on a Multi-Settlement Bank solution with J.P. Morgan (Kinexys), Deutsche Bank and Standard Chartered. The aim is to move settlement liquidity 24/7 without pre-funded nostro accounts. Production is targeted for Q1 2027. Separately, Deutsche Bank made its first euro cross-border payment on Partior in September 2025, with DBS as beneficiary, and cited more efficient reconciliation as a benefit.

**Thesis tie:** Neutral ordering and reconciliation across boundaries. A bank-owned network still brings in an independent market infrastructure to sit between "independent payment ecosystems".

**Post angle:** A shared ledger owned by banks went looking for a neutral third party to connect it to other payment ecosystems. Neutrality is being added on purpose as its own layer.

**Caution:** Not yet live (Q1 2027 target). Keep the claims to the announcement.
