# Dubai Land Department tokenised property pilot

**What happened:** DLD launched fractional property investment from AED 2,000 through Prypco Mint, with VARA, the UAE Central Bank, Dubai Future Foundation and Zand Digital Bank. The first project sold to 224 investors from 44 nationalities, and DLD then issued a Property Token Ownership Certificate. DLD projects tokenised assets could reach 7% of Dubai's market (AED 60 billion) by 2033. Search results report a secondary-trading phase from 20 February 2026 (not opened).

**Thesis tie:** Record bundled with governance. The land register remains the authority; the token platform, the regulator and the bank each hold part of the picture, and the certificate is how DLD reconciles them.

**Post angle:** Dubai put tokens on property but kept the land department as the issuer of the ownership certificate. The registry stayed sovereign; the new rail reports to it.

**Caution:** High risk of drifting into token or price talk. Never mention returns, yields or ledger names (press reports name the XRP Ledger; DLD's own release does not).
