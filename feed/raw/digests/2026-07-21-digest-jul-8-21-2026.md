# Digest — Jul 8–21, 2026

**TL;DR:** 3 calls (NVIDIA, USC, CIVICS.com). Agreement that the trust/interoperability problem is real and timely; two people independently pushed for concrete agent scenarios and research-scoped deliverables rather than vision.

Period: Jul 8–21, 2026 · Calls covered: 3
Three external calls with pipeline contacts were covered: Zhaozhong Ni (NVIDIA, Jul 9), Abba Wada (USC, Jul 20) and Dazza Greenwood (CIVICS.com, Jul 21; dates are recording storedAt in UTC). The headline: the outside reactions agree the trust and interoperability problem is real and timely, but two people independently pushed for concrete agent-centric use cases and for scoping the work as research deliverables rather than a vision.
## Trust, interoperability and concrete scenarios
- Zhaozhong Ni (NVIDIA, AI infrastructure): agrees the foundation problem is trust, but says "trust is more like your goal" and that below it is compatibility: "how do we even understand each other? How do they talk to each other?" He said blockchain/Web3 gave a first shot at that over the last 10 years.
- Zhaozhong Ni: "AI agent needs a way to talk to each other and there's no better venue than the blockchain or Web3 systems... So I buy that general concept." He still wanted specifics: "I understand the trust and the interoperability is important, but these are more like vision and what I call it, the issues."
- Zhaozhong Ni: asked OV to separate three interaction types: human to human (useful, but "it will never scale because human don't really scale"), agent to agent ("a huge, huge, huge workload"), and agent to human (whether a trusted agent can talk to other agents on his behalf). He asked for OV's thoughts on the scenarios "in between" the vision and the issues.
- Dazza Greenwood (CIVICS.com, MIT Media Lab, moving to Stanford): framed OV's direction as cross boundary interoperability (jurisdictions, networks, payment systems, legal systems, security boundaries): "the players can't fire each other... you've got to agree on stuff somehow." He said it "resonates" and is "pretty interesting, relevant, timely stuff", though he had more questions.
- Dazza Greenwood: sees promising research on agent-to-agent systems and on payments, especially "the transactions that the payments are paying for."
## Architecture: standard vs chain, event log vs state and compute
- Zhaozhong Ni: compared OV's approach to Bitcoin versus Ethereum. Bitcoin is "a transaction log... no state... no account balance", while EVM-style chains have state and compute and are "better for execution". He noted OV is logging events without doing computation, and reacted positively to that framing.
- Zhaozhong Ni: asked whether OV is a standard rather than a particular chain, with multiple interoperable implementations. He said the mechanism was fine and the idea worth examining, with details and benefiting scenarios still to be worked out.
## Zero-knowledge and post-quantum
- Abba Wada (recent USC graduate, USC Blockchain Club contact): enthusiastic about the zero-knowledge, post-quantum, tokenless direction: "I've been into zero knowledge proofs as long as I've known crypto" and "that'd be really sick to see if that's possible."
- Abba Wada: mentioned two friends in the USC club, PhD students "just focused on post quantum cryptography", and offered to reach out about their interest.
## Academic and student engagement, program design
- Dazza Greenwood: proposed two shapes. (1) An event: the MIT Summer Intensive on August 20, open and free, mostly hack sessions with working code and more abstract architecture or risk-model work, with OV-funded researchers and OV people serving on a feedback panel. (2) A research engagement from about September through December (possibly into mid-January): open research led by him with students and collaborators from Stanford, UC Berkeley and old MIT contacts, ending in a deliverable plus a couple of workshops that feeds back into an OV workstream. He asked whether about $50k was appropriate; OV said it was reasonable subject to scope. He also floated a one-year postdoc but cautioned "it sounds like you might not even be really ready for that yet."
- Dazza Greenwood: "to me it's not really worth doing unless it's truly useful." He suggested flexible direct support such as prizes, hackathon awards, microgrants or work-study. Agreed to a follow-up on Friday 11 to 12 with a shared Google Doc to define scope.
- Abba Wada: the USC club already runs company-funded grant and project arrangements, which he called a "great opportunity", so the proposed roughly $20k student consulting engagement fits a familiar format. He said OV's willingness to sponsor events and engage interdisciplinary students would be welcomed and would help spread the message on campus.
- Abba Wada: offered to introduce OV to the new club presidents, and to distribute a blurb from Callum in the club Slack and report responses. He cannot commit to a student ambassador role as he has graduated. He also asked about hiring and internship opportunities, and said he is recruiting for Web3 roles himself.
- Zhaozhong Ni: asked for public documents, notes or a primer, and offered to come back with what interests him.
## Open questions for R&D
1. What concrete scenarios does the layer enable across human-to-human, agent-to-agent and agent-to-human interactions (Zhaozhong Ni)?
1. Can a trusted agent represent a human to other agents, and how is that established (Zhaozhong Ni)?
1. What sits beneath trust: how do heterogeneous systems achieve compatibility and mutual understanding (Zhaozhong Ni)?
1. Is OV a standard with multiple interoperable implementations rather than a single chain (Zhaozhong Ni)?
1. Where does the event-log-without-state-or-computation design draw the line against EVM-style execution (Zhaozhong Ni)?
1. What are the specific cross boundary agreements needed, and what research on agent-to-agent payments and the transactions they fund should start first (Dazza Greenwood)?
1. What open research questions and challenge areas can be posed to hack-session or student participants (Dazza Greenwood, Abba Wada)?
