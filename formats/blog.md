# Format: blog (long-form article)

Cadence: one per week during the dry run.

## Structure

1. Open on a concrete scene or claim — a case, a number with its condition, or a
   line from the bank. Never open with a question to the reader.
2. What happened / what is true. Credit first where an existing system or
   community did something well.
3. Why it matters to the thesis — tie to the current storybook chapter and its
   open question.
4. Close on one concrete next step (read the two-pager, join the initiative,
   bring a question). One step, never a menu.

## Rules

- Voice per `voice/` guidelines; the retire list in
  `content-bank/lines/retire-or-handle-with-care.md` is binding.
- Numbers keep their conditions.
- 600–900 words. No summary conclusion paragraph.
