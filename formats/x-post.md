# Format: x-post

Cadence: two per week during the dry run.

## Structure

- Single post, 280 characters or fewer.
- One idea per post: a claim, a case with its number, or a quotable line from
  the bank.
- If it references a case, the post stands alone without the link.

## Rules

- Voice per `voice/` guidelines; the retire list in
  `content-bank/lines/retire-or-handle-with-care.md` is binding.
- No hashtags, no emoji, no "🧵", no engagement bait.
- Serves the current storybook chapter; a post may pose the chapter's question
  but must not answer it with a slogan.
